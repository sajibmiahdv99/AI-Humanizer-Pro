# AI Humanizer Pro — Store Listing

> One-line pitch: **Paste AI slop, get back copy that sounds like a real person — in your brand's voice, with a measurable confidence score.**

## Tagline
Make ChatGPT/Copilot text sound human in seconds — with voice presets, brand-voice matching, and an AI-confidence report.

## What it does
AI writes in a telltale pattern. AI Humanizer Pro finds every one of those tells (34 of them), rewrites the text to sound natural and human, and then **scores how AI-sounding it was — before and after** — so you can prove the edit worked.

## Why people pay for this
- **It's instant and hosted.** No Claude Desktop, no MCP config, no local setup. Paste text, get humanized copy.
- **8 voice presets** — casual, corporate, sales, academic, journalist, technical, support, default. Write in the tone your audience expects.
- **Brand-voice profiles** — feed it your writing sample or tone guide, and it locks onto your voice instead of a generic one.
- **Confidence report** — a before/after AI-confidence score plus "remaining tells," so you're never publishing blind.
- **No more AI-slop shame** — blog posts, emails, tweets, landing pages, resumes, docs. Paste in, publish confidently.
